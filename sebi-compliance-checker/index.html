Reg. 30 — event-triggered disclosures

not calendar-based
30′Board outcome

Board meeting outcomes

Disclose within 30 minutes of the closure of the board meeting — financial results, dividend, fundraising decisions, and other outcomes specified in Para A of Schedule III.

3hAfter trading hours

Board outcome after the close of trading

Where the board meeting closes after trading hours and more than three hours before the next trading day opens, the outcome may be disclosed within three hours of closure instead of thirty minutes.

12hWithin the company

Events originating within the company

Decisions taken in a meeting other than a board meeting (e.g., by a committee) must be disclosed within 12 hours of that meeting.

24hOutside the company

Events not originating within the company

Litigation, regulatory action, or another event outside the company's own decision-making must be disclosed within 24 hours of the company becoming aware of it.

24hReg. 30(11)

Market rumour verification

Confirm or deny a reported rumour within 24 hours, once material. Applicable to the top 100 listed companies from Dec 2023, and extended to the top 250 from a later date.

Insider trading — PIT Regulations, 2015

the compliance officer's standing job
SDDReg. 3(5) & 3(6)

Structured Digital Database

An internal database recording the nature of every piece of unpublished price sensitive information and the names and PANs of everyone who shared it and received it. It cannot be outsourced, and entries must be preserved for at least eight years.

7dRegulation 7(1)

Initial disclosure

On appointment as a director or KMP, or on becoming a promoter, the person discloses their holding of the company's securities within seven days.

2dRegulation 7(2)

Continual disclosure

Where a designated person's trades exceed ₹10,00,000 in value over any calendar quarter, they disclose within two trading days, and the company informs the exchanges within two trading days of receiving it.

9Regulation 9

Code of conduct & pre-clearance

Designated persons pre-clear trades, execute them within seven trading days of approval, and may not take a contra position for six months. The company's code must also maintain the Chinese wall between those with access to UPSI and the rest of the business.

Size-linked governance requirements

BRSRReg. 34(2)(f)

Business Responsibility & Sustainability Report

A structured ESG disclosure filed as part of the annual report — mandatory for the top 1,000 listed companies by market capitalisation.

RMCRegulation 21

Risk Management Committee

A board-level committee (majority independent/non-executive, at least one independent director) — mandatory for the top 1,000 listed companies, meeting at least twice a year.

DDPRegulation 43A

Dividend Distribution Policy

A disclosed board-approved policy on dividend distribution parameters, mandatory for the top 1,000 listed companies by market capitalisation.

118Sch. V, Part C

Annual Report — 21-day AGM notice

The annual report (including BRSR, where applicable) must reach shareholders at least 21 clear days before the AGM, and be filed with the stock exchanges no later than the day it is dispatched.

Disclaimer Reference tool built for portfolio purposes on the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (Regulations 13, 21, 23, 24A, 27, 30, 31, 33, 34, 43A and Schedule III), assuming a standard 1 April – 31 March financial year. Fine rates follow SEBI's Standard Operating Procedure for non-compliance with the LODR Regulations, levied by the stock exchange per day until compliance and subject to GST on top — a real exchange notice for a nine-day delay under Regulation 33 came to ₹45,000 plus 18% GST. Two items carry no published flat per-day rate and are marked accordingly rather than guessed at. Applicability thresholds, fine schedules and market-cap rankings shift periodically — confirm against SEBI's latest circulars and a practising Company Secretary before relying on this for an actual listed company.
