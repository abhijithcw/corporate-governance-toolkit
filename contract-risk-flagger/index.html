Disclaimer This is a pattern-matching triage aid, not legal advice and not a substitute for review by a practising Company Secretary or advocate. It matches text patterns — it does not read context, understand negation, or catch every risky drafting style, and it can both miss real risks and flag clauses that are actually fine in context. "Possible gap" items are absence-based guesses (something protective wasn't found nearby) and need a human read, not a confirmed finding.
