What switches on

Disclaimer Reference tool built for portfolio purposes on the Companies Act, 2013 and the rules made under it — the definition of a small company in Section 2(85), Sections 92, 134, 135, 138, 139, 149, 173, 177, 178, 203 and 204, and Rules 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 8A, 9 and 13 of the rules made under them. The small-company thresholds used here are ₹10 crore paid-up capital and ₹100 crore turnover, as substituted with effect from 1 December 2025 — a great deal of material still states the earlier ₹4 crore and ₹40 crore figures. Thresholds are applied on the figures you enter and take no account of the company's Articles, its history, or any exemption notification specific to it. The woman-director threshold under Rule 3 was not independently re-verified in the September 2026 pass and is marked accordingly.
