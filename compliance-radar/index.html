173Section

Board meetings

118Section · SS-2

General meeting minutes

Minutes of every general meeting go into the minutes book within 30 days and are signed by the chairman. SS-2 additionally expects the draft to be circulated to directors within 15 days of the meeting for their comments.

88Section

Statutory registers

Registers of members, directors & KMP, and charges must stay current at the registered office all year — these are checked, not filed, so they're easy to let slide between audits.

Conditional on your numbers — check before ruling out

135Section

CSR spend & reporting

Kicks in only if net worth ≥ ₹500 cr, turnover ≥ ₹1000 cr, or net profit ≥ ₹5 cr in the preceding year. Once triggered, at least 2% of average profit must be spent on CSR activity and reported annually — worth checking even for a private company that has scaled.

FLARBI · FEMA

Foreign liabilities & assets return

Any company with foreign investment (inbound or outbound) on its books must file this with the RBI by 15 July every year. It sits outside the Companies Act entirely, which is exactly why it's the most commonly missed filing among companies with foreign shareholders.
