Disclaimer Reference tool built for portfolio purposes on the Companies Act, 2013 and rules made under it, and on FEMA/RBI reporting requirements. Filing windows, additional-fee slabs and the charge-registration regime were checked against the bare provisions and the Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014 — in particular Section 42(8) for the fifteen-day return of allotment, Section 77 for the thirty / sixty / further sixty day charge windows, and the current additional-fee slabs. Fee figures are the Registrar's additional fee and do not include the normal filing fee, which varies with authorised capital, nor GST or professional charges. Several of these timelines have been amended in recent years and study material can lag — confirm against the current provision and a practising Company Secretary before relying on this for an actual filing.
